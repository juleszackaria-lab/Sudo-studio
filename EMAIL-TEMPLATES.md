## Email Best Practices

### **Subject Lines**

✅ **Good:**
- "Save $2,400/year on AI coding tools"
- "100% local AI development platform"
- "Your code. Your machine. Your privacy."

❌ **Avoid:**
- "Newsletter #47"
- "You won't believe this!"
- ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation!!!

### **Personalization**

✅ **Use:**
- First name
- Company name
- Specific pain points
- Relevant use cases

❌ **Avoid:**
- Generic "Dear Customer"
- Irrelevant features
- One-size-fits-all

### **Call to Action**

✅ **Clear CTAs:**
- "Download Now"
- "Schedule Demo"
- "Start Free Trial"
- "Purchase License"

❌ **Vague CTAs:**
- "Learn More"
- "Click Here"
- Multiple competing CTAs

### **Timing**

✅ **Best times:**
- Tuesday-Thursday
- 10am-11am or 2pm-3pm
- Avoid Mondays and Fridays

### **Follow-Up Cadence**

- **Day 0:** Initial outreach
- **Day 3:** First follow-up (if no response)
- **Day 7:** Second follow-up (if no response)
- **Day 14:** Final follow-up (if no response)
- **After demo:** Next day follow-up

## Tracking Metrics

### **Key Metrics to Monitor**

📊 **Open Rate** - Target: 20-30%  
📊 **Click Rate** - Target: 2-5%  
📊 **Reply Rate** - Target: 5-10% (cold), 20-30% (warm)  
📊 **Conversion Rate** - Target: 1-3% (cold), 10-20% (warm)  

### **A/B Test**

Test these variables:
- Subject lines
- Email length (short vs. long)
- CTA placement
- Personalization level
- Tone (formal vs. casual)
