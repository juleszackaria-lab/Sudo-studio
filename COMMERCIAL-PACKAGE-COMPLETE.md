- **Value:** Compliance, trust, security

### **2. Cost (Massive Savings)**
- ✅ **Us:** $299/year for whole team
- ❌ **Cursor:** $2,400/year for 10 devs (87% more expensive)
- **Value:** ROI in first year, budget friendly

### **3. Completeness (Platform vs. Tool)**
- ✅ **Us:** Chat + Diagnostics + SDK + DevOps + Environment
- ❌ **Copilot/Cursor:** Primarily chat + autocomplete
- **Value:** All-in-one solution, fewer tools needed

### **4. Control (Open Source)**
- ✅ **Us:** Full source code, MIT license, extensible
- ❌ **Copilot/Cursor:** Proprietary, black box
- **Value:** No vendor lock-in, customizable

### **5. Offline (Independence)**
- ✅ **Us:** Full functionality offline
- ❌ **Copilot/Cursor:** Requires constant internet
- **Value:** Work anywhere, reliable
